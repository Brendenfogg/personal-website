CGCS Command Hub

One browser tab for running a college civic center. The Command Hub puts the director's email, calendar, team, objectives, and the center's standard operating procedures on a single dashboard, with an AI assistant that answers from the SOP first.

Center for Government and Civic Service, Austin Community College · 2026 · JavaScript, Tailwind, Cloudflare Pages, Google Calendar and Gmail APIs, Claude API

Demo

What it does

  • Dashboard. The day's schedule, recent email, open objectives, and weekly reports on one screen.
  • Inbox and triage. Live Gmail with important mail first, plus a triage view that flags open loops: threads waiting on a reply for more than 72 hours, or from leadership.
  • AI assistant. Answers in two parts: what the center's SOP says, with the section cited, then broader advice. It can also look up email, the calendar, objectives, and people.
  • Team. Objectives, student employees, and task assignment with a workload view for each person.
  • Weekly reports. Student employees' weekly activity reports, organized by AI into themes, problems, and follow-ups.
  • People and network. A directory of contacts tagged by what they can help with, searchable by need.
  • Role-based views. Separate views for the director, the deputy, and student employees, for a team of eight.

How it's built

  • A single-page app in plain JavaScript and Tailwind, hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
  • Cloudflare Pages Functions keep Google sign-in alive between visits, so the director signs in once instead of every time.
  • AI requests go through the server, so no API key lives in the browser.
  • Google Calendar and Gmail connect through Google sign-in (OAuth).

Budget audit

Alongside the dashboard, I led a financial audit of the center's annual budget. It surfaced untracked expenses and revenue, and I designed a new budget and ledger system to close the gaps.

Built during my internship at the Center for Government and Civic Service.
