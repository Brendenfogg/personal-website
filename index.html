Hello! I'm Brenden.

I study Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan and work as an AI Analyst at Syzygy Services.

I'm interested in how businesses actually run, and how engineering can make them run better. The problems I care about live in the gap between how a process is supposed to work and how it works on a busy day: the extra steps, the missing numbers, the decisions made on instinct because the data isn't there.

My approach is the IOE toolkit. Start with the people doing the work, measure what is really happening, then use data, optimization, and well-built software to remove waste and make good decisions easier. I'm looking for work in operations, process improvement, and analytics where that moves the numbers that matter: cost, time, quality, and the experience of the people involved.
