Order Check

Personal project · 2026

An AI tool that listens while an order is entered and flags the ticket when it doesn't match what the customer said. The person stays in charge; the AI only points at the mismatch.

I tested it on 1,500 checks across 16 synthetic voices and three noise levels. It caught 96% of mistakes on the orders I tuned it with and 81% on a held-out set, where false alarms rose to 35%. I report the harder numbers, and the case study explains why.

Rust · Whisper speech recognition · SQLite
